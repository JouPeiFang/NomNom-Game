About the project

What it is

An offline 2D mini-game app designed for German 1st-graders to practice number recognition and quantity understanding — entirely through visuals, audio, and animation.

The goal

Developed in 4 months with a target of 80% task completion during testing. Curriculum-aligned with German 1st-grade math standards covering numbers 1–10.

Who it's for

1st-grade students (ages 6–7), played with support from teachers and parents. Sessions last 5–10 minutes — short enough to use as a classroom warm-up or reward.

Key philosophy

Minimal text, maximum visual clarity. Feedback through sounds and animations. No scores, no timers, no accounts — stress-free and encouraging for young learners.

Mini-games
🤚
Counting Hands

Players observe hand gesture images showing a number, then select the matching digit from 1–10. Correct answers turn green with a success sound; wrong ones turn red and stay visible. After 5 consecutive correct rounds, a celebration animation plays.

Number recognition Infinite rounds Visual feedback Streak system
🧌
Feeding the Monster

NomNom the monster asks for food! Players read a visual recipe showing required ingredients and quantities, drag the correct items into a basket, then drag the finished sandwich to NomNom's mouth. The monster reacts with a happy animation.

Quantity understanding Drag & drop Recipe logic Scene flow
Design principles
🎯
Simplicity

Visual-first design with icons and sounds — no reading required to navigate or play

🧱
Modularity

Each scene is an independent Godot scene — easy to expand with more mini-games later

⚡
Performance

Optimized sprites and audio; uses Godot's native features for smooth play on tablets and PCs

🔒
Privacy by design

No accounts, no data collected, fully offline — compliant and safe for school use

📚
Curriculum aligned

Content matches German 1st-grade numeracy goals — numbers 1–10, counting, quantities

🎉
Positive reinforcement

Confetti, sounds, and happy monster animations reward correct actions without penalties
